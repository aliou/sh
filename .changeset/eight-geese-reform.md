Tokenize newlines as first-class tokens and make separator handling match bash. The tokenizer now emits a `newline` token instead of smuggling line breaks as `op ";"` tokens, and one parameterized skip helper replaces the three separator-skipping loops. As a result the parser now rejects inputs bash rejects — `foo; ; bar`, `foo\n; bar`, `;;` outside `case`, a `;` after `&` (except the `;;` case terminator), a stray `;` where a statement, loop condition, or case-item body was expected — and accepts linebreak positions bash allows, such as `! # c\nfoo`, `x() # c\n{ foo; }`, and `foo & ;; esac`. zsh's lax separators keep working (`foo; ; bar`, `a=(1;2)`, `$(foo; ; bar)` parse in zsh mode). Missing operator continuations (`foo && # c`, `foo && ; bar`, `foo &&` at EOF) now report `` `&&` must be followed by a statement `` anchored at the operator's position — including real outer positions for operators inside `$(...)` — matching mvdan/sh, instead of a deep "Expected a command word". The parser was also split into focused modules under `src/parser/` (clauses, iterators, case, decl-let, test-clause, redirects, commands, words, and a shared parser-state module); behavior is unchanged apart from the fixes above.
